✦ PSYCHOLOGY × AI

The Psychology Behind Emotion

Emotion detection is not simply about recognizing a face. Human emotions are complex and influenced by many factors.

🧠
01

Emotions Are Complex

Emotional experiences can involve thoughts, physical responses, behaviour and the situation around a person.

👀
02

Context Matters

A facial expression does not always have one universal meaning. Context can change how an expression is interpreted.

😊
03

Faces Give Visual Clues

Facial movements can provide useful clues about emotional states, making them valuable for computer-vision research.

🤖
04

AI Makes an Estimate

An AI system predicts patterns learned from data. It cannot directly read someone's thoughts or know their complete internal emotional experience.

💡
Important perspective

EmoSense is designed as an AI-assisted estimation system. Its prediction should not be treated as a perfect measurement of a person's psychological state.
